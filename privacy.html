The short version

How long we keep it

  • Your account data and cloud bots: until you delete your account. Deleting removes everything immediately and for good.
  • Server backups may still contain your data for up to 14 days after deletion; they are then overwritten. Backups are used only to restore the server after a failure.
  • Chat messages: 7 days, then they are deleted automatically. A copy of a reported message is kept until the report has been handled, at most 12 months.
  • Friends, party and clan: until you remove them, leave, or delete your account. If you delete your account as a clan leader, the clan passes to another member.
  • Login sessions: 30 days after your last use. One-time codes: until used or expired.
  • Accounts that have not been used for 3 years may be deleted after we notify you by email.
  • If we shut down the online services, we will announce it in advance and then delete all account data.

Age

You need to be at least 14 years old to create an account (Art. 8 GDPR together with Art. 2-quinquies of the Italian Privacy Code). If you are younger, a parent or legal guardian must agree to the account for you.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format (Art. 15–21 GDPR). The fastest way: Download my data and Delete account on your account page. For anything else, write to ironmeltgame@gmail.com.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, for example the Italian Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

Security

Passwords are hashed with Argon2id, login tokens and one-time codes are stored only as hashes, all account traffic is encrypted (HTTPS), the database is not reachable from the internet, and repeated failed logins are slowed down. No system is perfectly secure; if we ever learn of a breach that affects your data, we will inform you and the authority as required by law.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters for you, we will tell you in the game or by email before it applies. The version number at the top changes with every update.
